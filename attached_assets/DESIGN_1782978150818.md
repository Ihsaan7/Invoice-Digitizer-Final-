## Brand & Style
The design system is engineered for efficiency, clarity, and professional trust. It is tailored for a web-based invoicing environment where data density must coexist with high legibility. 

The aesthetic follows a **Modern Corporate** movement: a refined take on minimalism that prioritizes structural integrity and information hierarchy. It avoids unnecessary decoration, instead using generous whitespace and a rigorous grid to guide the user’s eye through financial documents. The emotional response is one of reliability and "quiet authority"—providing a premium, stable experience for both the sender and the recipient of the invoice.

## Colors
This design system utilizes a high-contrast, professional palette designed for document clarity. 

- **Primary:** A deep, near-black charcoal used for primary text, borders, and structural elements to ground the interface.
- **Secondary:** A vibrant, amber-gold inspired by the Digitizer logo. This is used sparingly as a "functional accent" for call-to-actions, status indicators (e.g., "Pending"), or subtle highlights to draw attention to critical totals.
- **Tertiary:** A medium slate gray used for secondary information, meta-data, and placeholder text.
- **Neutral:** A range of cool grays and off-whites that create distinct "paper-like" surfaces for the invoice content, separating the document from the application background.

## Typography
The typography system is built on **Hanken Grotesk**, chosen for its sharp, contemporary geometry and exceptional legibility in professional contexts. 

- **Headlines:** Use Bold and Extra-Bold weights to create a clear hierarchy. Invoice numbers and "Total Due" amounts should use larger scale factors to ensure they are the first things noticed.
- **Data Display:** For tabular data (Quantities, Prices), use a tabular-numeral variant or a subtle monospaced font to ensure vertical alignment of digits.
- **Labels:** Use **Inter** in uppercase with increased letter spacing for small UI labels (e.g., "BILL TO", "DUE DATE") to distinguish them from the actual content data.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout employs a **12-column fixed grid** for the main workspace, centering the invoice "sheet" to mimic a physical document.

- **The "Sheet" Model:** On desktop, the invoice content is housed in a container with a fixed width (usually 800px) to maintain a classic A4/Letter aspect ratio, surrounded by a neutral application background.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** A strict 8px baseline grid is used to manage vertical spacing. Consistent "Stack" units ensure that the relationship between a label and its data is always tighter than the relationship between two different data sections.
- **Data Grids:** Tables should use a "comfortable" density by default, with 12px vertical padding on rows to ensure clear separation between line items.

## Elevation & Depth
In this design system, depth is communicated through **Tonal Layers** and **Low-Contrast Outlines** rather than heavy shadows.

- **Surface Levels:** The base application background is the lowest level (`Neutral`). The invoice "sheet" sits one level above, using a pure white background with a very subtle, 1px border (`#E5E7EB`).
- **Interaction Depth:** Hover states on list items or table rows use a subtle tint change (e.g., `Neutral-50`) rather than a shadow.
- **Focus:** Primary modals or dropdowns use a "Medium" ambient shadow—highly diffused (20px blur) with a low opacity (8%) to maintain the clean, corporate feel without appearing dated.

## Shapes
The shape language is **Soft** but disciplined. 

- **Elements:** Buttons and input fields use a 4px (0.25rem) corner radius. This provides a modern touch without sacrificing the professional "squareness" expected in financial software.
- **Large Components:** Cards and the main invoice sheet use an 8px (0.5rem) radius to define the primary workspace container.
- **Indicators:** Status chips (e.g., "Paid") use a pill-shape to distinguish them from actionable buttons.

## Components
- **Buttons:** Primary buttons use the Charcoal (Primary) color with white text. Secondary buttons use a ghost style with a 1px charcoal border. The Amber (Secondary) color is reserved for the "Send" or "Pay" button to drive conversion.
- **Data Tables:** Headers must have a subtle background tint and a solid 2px bottom border. Line items should have a 1px light gray divider between them.
- **Input Fields:** Use a "Float Label" pattern or a top-aligned label. The border should be a light gray, turning primary charcoal on focus.
- **Status Chips:** Small, pill-shaped indicators with light background tints and darker text (e.g., Light Green background with Dark Green text for "Paid").
- **Invoice Totals Block:** A dedicated component at the bottom right of the table, using `headline-md` for the final "Total Due" to ensure it is the most prominent piece of information on the page.
